What is UniVerify?

A quick overview before you start testing

UniVerify is a document authentication platform for Nigerian universities and institutions. When a university issues a certificate, transcript, or official letter, a unique QR code is embedded on the document. Anyone who scans that QR code can instantly verify the document is genuine.

This demo is running in test mode. No real money moves. You can test the full payment flow using the Paystack test card details provided below.

Generate a Document QR Code

Simulates what a university Registrar does when issuing a certificate
  1. Go to the Admin Dashboard at verify.akeenalee.com
  2. Fill in the Generate QR code panel on the left. Example: Title = "B.Sc Computer Science", Issued To = "John Doe", Issued By = "University of Lagos".
  3. Optionally set an ID prefix like UNILAG to see institution-specific IDs.
  4. Click Generate QR code. A QR code appears with a unique Document ID.
  5. Click Download PDF to see the full certificate with the QR code embedded.
  6. Note the Document ID (e.g. UNILAG-20260515-A3F9K2M1). You will need it for the next steps.
2

Verify as an Employer (Paid Flow)

Simulates what an employer or NYSC sees when they scan the QR code
  1. Take the verify URL from the QR result or construct it: verify.akeenalee.com/verify?doc=YOUR-DOC-ID
  2. Open the URL in your browser or scan the QR code with your phone camera.
  3. Since no student tokens exist yet, you will be redirected to the payment page.
  4. Enter any email address and click Pay and Verify.
  5. On the Paystack checkout page, enter the test card details from the box above.
  6. After payment you are redirected back and the Verified Authentic result is shown.
  7. Go back to the Admin Dashboard and check the Verification log. Your scan appears with timestamp, location, and payment method.
This replaces the WAEC/NECO scratch card with instant digital payment. No scratch card, no bank branch, no USSD code.
3

Register as a Student and Buy Tokens

Simulates what a graduate does before sharing their certificate with employers
  1. Go to the Student Portal at verify.akeenalee.com/student.html
  2. Click Register here and fill in your matric number, institution, and a 4-8 digit PIN.
  3. After registering you will see your portal showing 0 tokens.
  4. Click the Buy tokens tab and select a bundle.
  5. Enter an email for your receipt and click Proceed to payment.
  6. Use the test card details to complete payment on Paystack.
  7. You are redirected back. Your token balance updates immediately.
4

Verify Using a Student Token

When a student has tokens, verification is instant with no payment page
To link a document to your student record, the document must be generated with your matric number in the metadata field when creating the document via API.
  1. Make sure you are logged into the Student Portal with at least 1 token.
  2. Visit the verify URL for your document.
  3. The system checks your token balance first. One token is deducted and the verified result is shown immediately with no payment page.
  4. Go to your Student Portal and click the Verification log tab to see the scan logged.
5

View the Audit Log

Every scan is logged. The institution sees everything.
  1. Go to the Admin Dashboard.
  2. Scroll down to the Verification log table.
  3. Every scan appears with document name, result, payment method, IP address, location, device type, and exact timestamp.
  4. Click Refresh to pull the latest entries. The log auto-refreshes every 30 seconds.
  5. The three stat cards at the top show total scans, scans today, and total documents registered.
The institution sees every employer who checked a document, from which country, on which device, and whether they paid or used a student token.
